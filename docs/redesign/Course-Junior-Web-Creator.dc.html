Questions about Junior Web Creator

Does my child need any coding experience?

No. This course is for complete beginners aged 8 to 12.

What do they need at home?

A laptop or desktop computer with an internet connection.

Group or self-paced: which is better for my child?

Most children this age do best in live group classes, where a tutor keeps them on track. Self-paced suits confident learners with a parent nearby to help.

Can we try it first?

Yes. The first interactive class is free and you don't need a card.

Ready to build their first website?

Pick live group classes or self-paced, and get started.
