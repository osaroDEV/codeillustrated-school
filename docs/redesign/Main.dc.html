Questions parents ask

Is the first class really free?

Yes. Your first interactive class is free and you don't need a card. You only pay if you decide to enrol.

What's the difference between group classes and self-paced?

Group classes are live with a tutor and a small group, on a set weekly schedule, billed monthly. Self-paced gives you all the lessons and selected class recordings with no live classes, billed monthly or yearly.

We're not in the same country as you. What time are classes?

We teach learners in many countries. Class times on our site are shown in your own time zone, and your schedule email uses your local time too.

What currency will I pay in?

Prices show in naira (₦) in Nigeria and US dollars ($) everywhere else. You pay securely by card.

Can we cancel?

Yes, any time from your dashboard.
